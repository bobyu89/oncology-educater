## 1. AJCC/UICC 9th edition (IASLC) lung cancer TNM

### 1.1 New descriptors [VERIFIED]

The T categories are unchanged from the 8th edition. M1a and M1b are also unchanged.

| Descriptor | 9th edition definition (IASLC wording) |
|---|---|
| **N2a** | Metastasis in a **single** ipsilateral mediastinal **or** subcarinal nodal station |
| **N2b** | Metastasis in **multiple** ipsilateral mediastinal nodal stations, with or without involvement of the subcarinal station |
| **M1c1** | **Multiple** extrathoracic metastases in **one organ system** |
| **M1c2** | Multiple extrathoracic metastases in **multiple organ systems** |

Notes:
- The count is of nodal **stations**, not individual nodes.
- Per Radiology Assistant (secondary source): a paired organ counts as one organ system. Multiple bone lesions only, or multiple skin lesions only, are M1c1. Bone plus adrenal is M1c2.
- Both M1c1 and M1c2 remain **stage IVB**. M1a and M1b remain **stage IVA**.
- IASLC has validated the 9th edition for SCLC, including N2a/N2b and M1c1/M1c2 (Tsao et al., 2025). The 9th edition therefore applies to SCLC and other neuroendocrine carcinomas too.

## 3. NCI CTCAE: v5.0 or v6.0?

**v6.0 is current** **[VERIFIED]**.
- The title page of the NCI CTCAE v6.0 PDF reads: "Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v6.0 (MedDRA 28.0) — **Published July 22, 2025**".
  - Source: https://dctd.cancer.gov/research/ctep-trials/for-sites/adverse-events/ctcae-v6.pdf
  - The PDF metadata shows a modification date of 2026-01-20, which is consistent with a reported January 2026 errata. I did not see an errata notice on an NCI page itself, so the errata detail is **[PARTIAL]**.
- The NCI "Adverse Events" page (updated 2026-09-23) says: "The current Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v6.0 was released in 2025."
  - CTCAE v6.0 is distributed as an Excel file (with tracked changes and a v5.0→v6.0 mapping) plus a PDF quick reference.
  - CTCAE v5.0 (2017, MedDRA 20.1) remains posted.
  - Source: https://dctd.cancer.gov/research/ctep-trials/for-sites/adverse-events

**Transition status** **[VERIFIED: what each source says; the two NCI pages differ on timing]**
- The **CTCAE v6.0 Implementation FAQ** (dated 9 September 2025) says:
  - v6.0 is "ready for immediate use for any study except NCI CTEP or DCP NCORP studies."
  - "All NCI CTEP/DCP studies currently reporting in CTCAE v5.0 will continue to report in CTCAE v5.0 throughout the life of the study". No conversion is required.
  - v6.0 is required for all **new** CTEP/DCP studies whose Rave build begins after Rave ALS 7.2 is released. That release was "tentatively scheduled for July 2026".
  - CTCAE v7.0 is expected between 2027 and 2030.
  - Source: https://dctd.cancer.gov/research/ctep-trials/for-sites/adverse-events/ctcae-v6-faq.pdf
- The CTEP trial-development page says: "Implementation for CTEP/DCP studies is targeted for January 1, 2026. It will apply to newly opened studies only." This conflicts with the FAQ's July 2026 Rave timeline, so I report both.
  - Source: https://ctep.cancer.gov/protocoldevelopment/electronic_applications/ctc.htm
- **Practical summary for nurses.** New trials are moving to v6.0. Ongoing trials keep v5.0 for their whole life. The protocol decides which version applies.
- No NCI statement mandates a change in routine clinical-practice grading. Whether Taiwanese hospitals have switched to v6.0 is **[UNVERIFIED]**.

**Key changes in v6.0 [PARTIAL: secondary sources]**
- Grading of lab adverse events relative to an abnormal **baseline**.
- Better characterization of immunotherapy adverse events. v6.0 includes "Cytokine release syndrome" and "Immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome"; I confirmed both terms in the v6.0 PDF.
- Refined cardiac categories.
- Many skin terms graded by functional or psychosocial impact instead of BSA.

Sources for these changes:
- NCI DCTD news: https://dctd.cancer.gov/about/news-events/news/2026-news/ctcae-updated-version-6
- ONS Voice, 9 July 2026: https://www.ons.org/publications-research/voice/news-views/07-2026/heres-what-updated-ctcae-version-60-means-oncology-nurses
- Gulati, N., Block, B. R., Mehta, J., & Marron, T. U. (2026). A dermatologist's perspective on cutaneous adverse effects in CTCAE version 6.0. *JAMA Oncology, 12*(7), 689–690. https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2026.1043

## 5. HPA lung cancer early detection program (肺癌早期偵測計畫, LDCT)

### 5.1 Original program: started 2022-07-01 (111年7月1日) [VERIFIED]

Source: MOHW release 「開辦一個月；2,000人接受篩檢」, filed 111-08-22. https://mohw.gov.tw/cp-16-71105-1.html

Taiwan has called itself the first country to include **family history** in a national screening program. Original eligibility:
- **Family history:** men 50–74 or women 45–74 whose parent, child or sibling (父母、子女或兄弟姊妹) had lung cancer.
- **Heavy smokers:** age 50–74, ≥**30 pack-years**, willing to quit or quit within 15 years (有意願戒菸或戒菸15年內).
- **Interval:** free LDCT once every 2 years.

### 5.2 Current criteria: expansion effective 2025-01-01 (114年1月1日) [VERIFIED]

Sources:
- MOHW release 「114年擴大肺癌篩檢服務 新年從健康開始」 (filed 114-01-10): https://mohw.gov.tw/cp-16-81184-1.html
- MOHW release 2025-04-08: https://mohw.gov.tw/cp-16-82095-1.html
- The criteria are repeated unchanged in the MOHW release of 2025-12-30 (cp-2704-84987-1).

Current eligibility:
1. **Family history (具肺癌家族史):** **men 45–74 or women 40–74**, whose blood-related **parents, children or siblings** (有血緣關係之父母、子女或兄弟姊妹; first-degree relatives only) were diagnosed with lung cancer.
   - The age limits were lowered by 5 years: men from 50 to 45, women from 45 to 40.
   - Smokers in this group should also receive smoking-cessation services.
2. **Heavy smokers (重度吸菸者):** age **50–74**, smoking history **≥20 pack-years**, still smoking **or quit less than 15 years ago** (仍在吸菸或戒菸未達15年).
   - The threshold was lowered from 30 to 20 pack-years, following USPSTF.
   - Pack-years = packs per day × years smoked. For example, 1 pack a day for 20 years, or half a pack a day for 40 years.
3. **Interval:** free chest LDCT **once every 2 years**. The person must hold National Health Insurance (具健保身分). Non-high-risk people should have a physician decide whether to screen.

**2026 changes:** none found. **[PARTIAL: absence of evidence]** HPA's own site was not reachable. MOHW and news sources up to March 2026 still describe the 2025 criteria.

### 5.3 Program yield [VERIFIED for MOHW; PARTIAL for news]

- **To 2024-12-31** (MOHW): 158,937 people screened; **1,957 lung cancers** found; **83% early stage (0–I)**, which is 1,621 cases.
- **To 2025-12-31** (HPA Director-General quoted by UDN, 2026-03-17): **277,306 screenings** (人次); **3,139 confirmed lung cancers**; detection rate about **1.25%**. https://udn.com/news/story/124789/9384489

## 6. TALENT trial [VERIFIED]

Chang, G.-C., Chiu, C.-H., Yu, C.-J., Chang, Y.-C., Chang, Y.-H., Hsu, K.-H., Wu, Y.-C., Chen, C.-Y., Hsu, H.-H., Wu, M.-T., Yang, C.-T., Chong, I.-W., Lin, Y.-C., Hsia, T.-C., Lin, M.-C., Su, W.-C., Lin, C.-B., Lee, K.-Y., Wei, Y.-F., … Yang, P.-C. (2024). Low-dose CT screening among never-smokers with or without a family history of lung cancer in Taiwan: A prospective cohort study. *The Lancet Respiratory Medicine, 12*(2), 141–152. https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-2600(23)00338-7

- PMID 38042167. Epub 2023-11-29. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02611570.
- APA 7 lists the first 19 authors, then an ellipsis, then the last author (Yang, P.-C.).
- The authors' reply to correspondence is a separate item: *Lancet Respir Med* 2024;12(4):e15, https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-2600(24)00037-7, PMID 38430928.

**Design.** Nationwide prospective cohort at 17 tertiary centres in Taiwan.
- Enrolment: 1 December 2015 to 31 July 2019.
- Age 55–75 with a negative chest X-ray.
- Never-smokers, or those who smoked under 10 pack-years and quit more than 15 years ago.
- At least one risk factor: family history of lung cancer; passive smoking; history of tuberculosis or COPD; cooking index ≥110; or cooking without ventilation.
- Positive scan: solid or part-solid nodule >6 mm, or pure ground-glass nodule >5 mm.

**Key results (1-year follow-up after baseline LDCT):**
- 12,011 participants (8,868 women); 6,009 had a family history of lung cancer.
- Positive baseline scans: 2,094 (17.4%).
- **Lung cancer detection rate: 2.6%** (318/12,011).
  - Invasive cancer: 2.1% (257).
  - Adenocarcinoma in situ: 0.5% (61).
- 317 of 318 cancers were adenocarcinoma.
- **Stage I: 77.4%** (246/318). The abstract reports stage I only; it does not give a combined "stage 0–I" figure. Counting AIS (stage 0) separately is not possible from the abstract.
- Invasive cancer with a family history: 2.7% (161/6,009). Without a family history: 1.6% (96/6,002).
- Independent risk factors: female sex, family history, age over 60. The risk rises with the number of affected first-degree relatives, and is higher when the mother or a sibling was affected.
- Passive smoking, cooking exposure, and prior lung disease were **not** associated with lung cancer.
- LDCT performance: sensitivity 92.1%, specificity 84.6%, PPV 14.0%, NPV 99.7%.
- The authors acknowledge possible overdiagnosis, especially for AIS.
- MOHW cites TALENT as the evidence for including family history in the national program.

### 7.1 PIONEER: Asian, advanced adenocarcinoma [VERIFIED]

Shi, Y., Au, J. S.-K., Thongprasert, S., Srinivasan, S., Tsai, C.-M., Khoa, M. T., Heeroma, K., Itoh, Y., Cornelio, G., & Yang, P.-C. (2014). A prospective, molecular epidemiology study of EGFR mutations in Asian patients with advanced non-small-cell lung cancer of adenocarcinoma histology (PIONEER). *Journal of Thoracic Oncology, 9*(2), 154–162. https://doi.org/10.1097/JTO.0000000000000033 (PMID 24419411; PMCID PMC4132036)

- Population: 1,482 patients with untreated stage IIIB/IV adenocarcinoma from 7 regions (mainland China, Hong Kong, India, Philippines, Taiwan, Thailand, Vietnam).
- **EGFR mutation overall: 51.4%** (746/1,450 evaluable).
  - Women 61.1%; men 44.0%.
  - Never-smokers 60.7%.
  - By region: India 22.2%, Vietnam 64.2%; the other regions 47.2–62.1%.
- **Taiwan: 62.1% (108/174) [PARTIAL]**. The full text gives "62.1% [108 of 174]" as the top of the "other countries" range but does not name the country there; the country table is an image. A secondary source (Boehringer Ingelheim handbook, found by web search) attributes 62.1% to Taiwan.

### 7.2 Taiwan-specific multicentre study, with ALK [VERIFIED]

Hsu, K.-H., Ho, C.-C., Hsia, T.-C., Tseng, J.-S., Su, K.-Y., Wu, M.-F., … Chang, G.-C. (2015). Identification of five driver gene mutations in patients with treatment-naïve lung adenocarcinoma in Taiwan. *PLoS ONE, 10*(3), e0120852. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0120852 (PMID 25789627; PMCID PMC4366385)

- Population: 1,772 patients with lung adenocarcinoma, prospective, August 2011 to November 2013.
- **EGFR 55.7%** (987/1,772). KRAS 5.2%; HER2 2.0%; BRAF 0.7%.
- **EML4-ALK: 9.8% of EGFR-wild-type patients** (29/295, Ventana IHC).
  - Applied to the EGFR-wild-type fraction, this is about **4%** of all adenocarcinomas (computed: 9.8% × 44.3% ≈ 4.3%).
  - Only EGFR-wild-type tumours were tested for ALK. State this when quoting the figure.
