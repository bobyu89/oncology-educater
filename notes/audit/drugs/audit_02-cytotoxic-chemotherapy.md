## Fixes (section, what was wrong, what it is now, source)

1. Header table, 1.1 (1946 row), section 11 entry and APA note: Goodman 1946 was cited as "Goodman & Wintrobe" with a note saying the authors followed PubMed and CrossRef. The paper has six authors (Goodman, Wintrobe, Dameshek, Goodman, Gilman, McLennan); CrossRef lists only Goodman. Now "Goodman et al., 1946", six-author entry, issue (3) added, note rewritten. Source: PubMed PMID 6368885 (JAMA 1984 Landmark reprint, title lists the six authors); CrossRef DOI record.
2. 1.1, 1946 row: "戰時保密解除" is not in any cited source. Now "第一批 nitrogen mustard 臨床結果公開發表" with Falzone 2018 cited. Source: Falzone 2018 full text.
3. 1.1, 1943 row: significance cell was an unsourced interpretation of the Alexander report (which has no abstract). Now states bone-marrow aplasia in the exposed (Falzone 2018) and the event's place in the origin story (Turner & Sapp 2026 abstract).
4. 1.1 (1974 row) and 1.3: "高劑量 methotrexate" was attributed to Jaffe 1974, whose PubMed record has no abstract and whose title does not say high-dose; no cited source supports it. "高劑量" removed from the Jaffe sentences; the link to MAP kept as methotrexate plus leucovorin rescue. Source: PubMed record PMID 4606174; Falzone 2018.
5. 1.1, note under the timeline: it said every approval year is the label's Initial U.S. Approval, but the Onivyde label gives 1996 (irinotecan). Note now states the exception and that 2015 comes from Thomas & Pommier 2019. Source: Onivyde label; Thomas & Pommier 2019 full text.
6. Section 1 opening: "來源只有四條路" contradicted sections 1.4 and 1.7. Now "主要有四條路" plus laboratory-synthesised analogues. Source: Falzone 2018; Hertel 1990 abstract.
7. 1.4 heading: "從設計出發的藥" asserted the design story that the chapter's own 待核對 box says was not verified. Now "實驗室合成的氟化嘧啶". Source: Falzone 2018 ("synthesized 5-FU"). No inbound links used the old anchor.
8. 1.2, first patient: "病歷上只寫 JD" was wrong; JD is how the literature refers to him. Also the chart wording was "a lymphocidal" or "substance X". Both corrected. Source: Christakis 2011 full text.
9. 1.6, podophyllin row: plant and resin were equated. Now "鬼臼類植物的樹脂（podophyllin）". Source: Hande 1998 abstract.
10. 2.2: "24% 對 5.3%" did not say which arm was which. Now "對照組 24%、CMF 組 5.3%". Source: Bonadonna 1976 abstract.
11. 3.1, mechanism sentence: said DNA cross-linking is the main action of cyclophosphamide, citing the label. The label says the mechanism is not fully characterised and cross-linking "may be involved". Sentence split by drug. Source: BluePoint cyclophosphamide label 12.1; Fresenius Kabi ifosfamide label 12.1.
12. 3.1 dacarbazine row and section 6 nausea row: "噁心嘔吐是最常見的毒性" conflicted with the boxed warning ("Hemopoietic depression is the most common toxicity"). Now gives both statements as the label words them, and calls the warning boxed. Source: Hikma dacarbazine label (boxed warning; Adverse Reactions).
13. 3.5, class mechanism sentence and epirubicin row: epirubicin had no mechanism stated and the class sentence cited only the doxorubicin label. Sentence now cites both labels; epirubicin row adds intercalation and topoisomerase II cleavage. Source: Ellence label 12.1; Pfizer doxorubicin label 12.1.
14. 3.6 nab-paclitaxel row and 4.2 liver row: said moderate to severe hepatic impairment needs a lower starting dose. For pancreatic cancer, the chapter's main use, the label says not recommended. Both places now say: lower starting dose for breast and lung, not recommended for pancreas. "肺炎" marked as pneumonitis. Source: Abraxane label Highlights and section 2.5.
15. 3.6 eribulin row: OS 13.5 vs 11.5 months (HR 0.77) was given as a liposarcoma result; it is the whole trial (liposarcoma plus leiomyosarcoma). Now attributed correctly, with the liposarcoma stratum (15.6 vs 8.4 months, HR 0.51) and the liposarcoma-only indication from the label. Source: Schöffski 2016 abstract; Halaven label sections 1.2 and 14.2. Matches sarcoma chapter 4.4.
16. Card, cyclophosphamide (mechanism cell): aligned with fix 11.
17. Card, epirubicin (mechanism cell): was only "Doxorubicin 的 4-epimer", which is not a mechanism; aligned with fix 13.

Quiz: no changes. All 16 keys are the single best answer and are supported by the text.
